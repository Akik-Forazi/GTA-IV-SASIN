# Clue & Reveal Schedule — GTA IV: SASIN

**Purpose:** enforce fair play. Every revelation spends only coins planted earlier. Distinguishes **player knowledge** from **character knowledge** at every step. Companion to `campaign-outline.md` and `writer_master_chronology.md`.

**Rules**
1. A clue's *surface reading* must be available to the player when shown.
2. A character may act on a clue only when their profile's knowledge boundary permits.
3. No reveal may depend on a fact absent from this table (canon 2.10 prohibitions).
4. "Sender" column refers to writer truth; characters may misread senders.

## Player-knowledge vs. writer-knowledge gates (spoiler timing)
- Player learns "a second watcher exists" (M3) → "the second watcher is the map's author" (M4) → "fabrications have a bookkeeper" (M11) → "the bookkeeper's name/face" (M14). Four gates, no skips.
- Player learns "a buyer existed" (M3/C-07–C-08) → "the buyer is a structural account" (M6/C-13) → "the buyer's name" (M12/C-19). Three gates.
- Niko's concealment: hinted (M1–M2) → phrase-echo burn (M3) → employment note (M5) → object shown (M11) → his full accounting (M16). The rupture is *earned across five touches*.
- Jae-Ho alive: suspected (M2/C-05) → confirmed (M10). The confession never precedes the suspicion.
- The trust: observable (M5/C-12) → delivered (M15/C-21). The "why now" is visible to attentive players a full act early — intended reward, not a leak.

## Fair-play audit notes
- No reveal uses information unavailable to the player at its gate (checked clue-by-clue against missions).
- The Accountant is *deducible* before he is shown: C-05 + C-15 + C-16 + C-17 all share one signature style — a player who adds them in M11 is meant to beat Min-ji to the deduction by one scene (rewarding attention).
- The Beneficiary is *not* guessable before M12 by design: no on-screen character connects Baek to anything before C-19 completes; the fair-play contract is that all five stack items were individually planted and shown.
