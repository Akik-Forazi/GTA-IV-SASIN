## PHASE 2: CHARACTER & MYSTERY (Stages 3-4)
### Stage 3: Develop the Characters ⏳ IN PROGRESS
- **Haru (SASIN):** Public identity: powerful, connected, dangerous; Private self: confused control vs. certainty; Arc: beyond revenge — confront what justice means; What he knows at each stage: starts wealthy/connected, gradually learns father's compromises; Arc: moves from inherited identity to chosen path
- **Kim Min-ji (Sister):** Public identity: corporate strategist controlling family business; Private self: believes controlling information prevents catastrophe; Arc: decide whether exposing compromised past destroys or saves the legitimate business; What she knows at each stage: starts believing in information control; gradually discovers father's deeper involvement; arc conclusion: exposes on her own terms through legal channels
- **Niko Bellic:** Public identity: guarded, practical; Private self: deciding what other people need to know; Central flaw: information control; What he knows: witnessed intermediary's identity during massacre; concealed fragment to protect survivors; emotional conflict: Haru feels betrayed; Niko feels he was protecting the only people he had left after Kate's death
- **Mandu (Con artist):** Public identity: irreverent humorist, deceiver; Private self: perceptive, capable of real loyalty; Flaw: turns serious situations into performances because sincerity makes him vulnerable; Arc: from useful deceiver to person willing to risk something real for chosen family; Personal stake: his family suffered losses connected to the same network (concealed beneath performance)
- **The Beneficiary/Mastermind:** Public identity: respected international business figure; Private self: believes everyone has a price; Strength: influence, leverage, information control; Blind spots: underestimates those who refuse to be bought; manipulates through deniable intermediaries, not direct action

### Stage 4: Construct the Mystery ⏳ PENDING
- **Goal:** Clue matrix mapping every major clue to: location/mission, surface interpretation, actual significance, discoverer, concealer, player deduction, supported revelation
- **Evidence categories to evaluate (from 8 possibilities):**
  1. Anonymous photograph related to the massacre
  2. Supposedly deceased intermediary whose transactions continued
  3. Missing corporate transaction shortly before the attack
  4. Conflicting records of weapons shipments or financial transfers
  5. Niko's concealed fragment of evidence
  6. Corporate acquisitions connected to former family assets
  7. Records showing father began dismantling part of the network
  8. Evidence of a final transaction whose consequences he misunderstood/underestimated
- **Selection criteria:** Choose 4-6 that create strongest causal chain without overloading narrative
- **Revelation requirements for each major reveal:** truth, who knows at start, false/incomplete belief, earlier clue, mission/event exposing it, meaning change, emotional consequences, what player can infer, remaining uncertainty
- **Prohibition:** No hidden twins, convenient amnesia, random last-minute witnesses, unexplained secret recordings, villain explaining entire conspiracy in one monologue

## PHASE 3: CAMPAIGN STRUCTURE (Stages 5-7)
### Stage 5: Outline the Campaign ⏳ PENDING
- **Target:** 15-18 substantial missions across 5 provisional acts
- **Act I — The Return (missions 1-3):** Being written now
- **Act II — The Family Empire (missions 4-6):** Haru reunites with sister; outside company acquires connected interests; Mandu uses deception; missing transaction from records
- **Act III — The Man Who Lied (missions 7-10):** Haru confronts Niko about concealed evidence; intermediary trail contradicts records; retaliation threatens outsiders; evidence identifies intermediaries but not mastermind
- **Act IV — The Beneficiary (missions 11-14):** Investigation reaches respected business figure; Haru/sister disagree on revenge/exposure; Mandu uncovers hidden record; connection between massacre and father's dismantling attempt
- **Act V — SASIN (missions 15-18):** Complete chain of decisions clear; father's compromises/final attempt revealed; sister decides how much to expose; Haru confronts mastermind; ending tests revenge/exposure/control choice
- **Per-mission requirements:** Clear dramatic objective, reason matters now, character conflict/development, playable variety with meaningful escalation, consequence, new information/changed relationship, memorable ending/reveal/decision
- **Variety:** Mix of surveillance, infiltration, investigation, deception, corporate maneuvering, pursuit, negotiation, stealth, combat, character-driven scenes
- **Causality check:** Every mission must advance at least one: central mystery, principal character arc, major relationship, consequential conflict, or endgame
