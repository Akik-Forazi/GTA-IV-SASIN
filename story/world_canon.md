# World Canon — GTA IV: SASIN

**Status: CONFIRMED (user-declared, 2026-10-11).** This file records world-level canon declared directly by the user. It binds every writer, mission script, cinematic, and environmental-design decision. Ledger cross-reference: canon-ledger §1a.

## W-1. Technological Stagnation (user declaration, preserved)

> **Present year:** 2025.
>
> The world of GTA IV: SASIN has undergone significant economic, criminal, corporate, and political evolution, but its everyday technology has stagnated around the technological era represented by GTA IV.
>
> This is not a world in which history stopped. Criminal networks have become more sophisticated. Corporations have consolidated influence. Wealth, surveillance practices, political leverage, and systems of control have evolved through human organization rather than a dramatic technological revolution.
>
> The visual and technological identity of Liberty City remains recognizably rooted in GTA IV's era. Period-appropriate phones, vehicles, interfaces, public infrastructure, and everyday devices remain natural parts of life in 2025.
>
> **Creative rule:** Modernize the power structures, not the technological aesthetic.
>
> Writers must preserve this distinction across missions, dialogue, props, interfaces, cinematics, and environmental storytelling. New technology must not appear merely because it exists in the real-world 2025; it must fit the established alternate history.

## W-2. What this means operationally

**The split every scene must respect:**

| Layer | Status in 2025 | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **Power structures** | Modernized through human organization | Consolidated corporations (Haeun, Seorin and successors), professionalized criminal networks with compliance-style tradecraft, political leverage, layered intermediaries, priced obligations, information control as doctrine |
| **Everyday technology** | Stagnated at GTA IV-era | Flip/early-slider phones with in-game UI, 2008-era vehicles and traffic, CRT-and-beige offices alongside early flat panels, fax and paper memos, physical records, phone books, newspaper notices, landline desks, burned CDs, camera shops |

**Rules of thumb for writers:**

1. **If a device would be invisible inside GTA IV's Liberty City, it is allowed.** If it would look like a product placement from the real-world 2020s, it is forbidden.
2. **Sophistication is human, not digital.** The network is dangerous because of disciplined hierarchy, compartmentalization, bought silence, and accounting rigor — not because of hacking, drones, or mass data collection.
3. **No convenience escapes.** A mystery may never be advanced or solved by anything GTA IV-era technology could not plausibly do: no cloud records, no social-media trails, no GPS logs as plot keys, no facial-recognition shortcuts, no "just search it online."
4. **Surveillance is grainy and human.** Watchers sit in sedans and photograph license plates. CCTV exists but is low-resolution, poorly retained, and physically retrieved. Someone always has to *watch*.
5. **Evidence is physical by default.** Photographs are prints from film rolls; documents are paper with signature blocks; communication leaves burner phones, prepaid cards, pager-style one-way nudges, and typed strips.

## W-3. Why this canon strengthens the campaign

- **Fair-play integrity.** The photograph chain (one roll, three prints), the burner text, the expense note, the sexton's paper ledger, and the trust's statutory notice all *depend* on a low-digital world. Physical evidence cannot be reverse-searched, so every clue must be carried by people — exactly the design the clue-schedule already implements.
- **The Accountant's method becomes plausible.** An auditing mind is terrifying in a paper world: he sees filings, notices, and invoices because there is no algorithm watching for him.
- **Min-ji's doctrine (information control) gains weight.** In a stagnated-tech world, information control is a *human* discipline — records custody, filing strategy, reputation management — which is precisely her corporate skill set.
- **Environmental storytelling.** A 2025 Liberty City of GTA IV-era devices visually signals the alternate history without a line of dialogue.

## W-4. Writer checklist (apply to every script and cinematic)

- [ ] No device, interface, app, or service post-dating GTA IV's era appears on screen or in dialogue.
- [ ] No plot beat relies on information moving faster or being stored more durably than GTA IV-era technology allows.
- [ ] Every instance of "modern" power (corporate, criminal, political) is expressed through organization, personnel, paperwork, and leverage — not technology.
- [ ] Props and environmental notes reference period items (phones, vehicles, media, signage, office equipment) naturally, without lampshading the year.
- [ ] When in doubt: the year is 2025, but the devices are the ones Liberty City already had.
