# Canon Ledger — GTA IV: SASIN

**This is the project's single source of truth.** Every creative or technical claim must be filed here under exactly one status. Nothing becomes canon by drifting into a mission script; a proposal becomes canon only when the user approves it here.

## Status definitions

- **CONFIRMED** — Explicitly established by the user, by GTA IV's canon, or accepted in an earlier user-approved decision. May not be changed without user approval.
- **PROPOSED** — A creative solution adopted provisionally by the writers to keep work moving. Clearly labeled wherever used. Needs user approval to become CONFIRMED. Consequential proposals are listed in the Decision Queue (bottom of this file).
- **OPEN** — An unresolved question. Writers may draft against a provisional working answer, but the answer stays PROPOSED until approved.
- **REJECTED** — A previously considered idea that must not return without a compelling new reason and user approval.

Writer-only material (true chronology, secrets, spoiler timing) lives in `writer_master_chronology.md` and `characters/` (writer sections). Player-facing documents must never import writer-only facts.
