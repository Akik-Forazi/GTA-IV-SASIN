# Campaign Outline — GTA IV: SASIN

**Structure:** 17 missions, 5 acts (within the 15–18 target). Every mission advances at least one of: central mystery, principal arc, major relationship, consequential conflict, endgame. No filler.

**How to read this file:** each mission lists Objective (dramatic), Gameplay (variety + implementable scope), Clue (fair-play placement), Consequence (tracked in `consequence-tracker.md`), and Transition. Full dialogue scripts exist for Act I; Acts II–V are written one mission at a time after approval (Readmap Stage 8 discipline).

**Status:** per `canon-ledger.md` — act/mission structure is PROPOSED (5.1); titles below are working titles.

## ACT I — THE RETURN (M1–M4)

### M1 — "The Return"
- **Objective:** Haru lands at Francis International Airport carrying print #1; reconnects with Niko and Roman; the watcher presence is established. *(Dramatic: inciting incident.)*
- **Gameplay:** walk/taxi from arrivals; optional Roman taxi ride; phone-call scene; safehouse arrival. No combat — tone-setter.
- **Clue:** the photograph itself (suited liaison, no name). The "—A." docks text arrives and is *declined* — Haru goes to the safehouse instead (control).
- **Consequence:** docks meet missed → M4's second lure. Watchers log his arrival.
- **Transition:** Roman mentions the depot's "new corporate account" (seed M8). Night ends.
- **Fixes vs. prior draft:** airport is Francis International (7.5); photo chain defined (one coherent chain per 4.9); Roman canon family added; garbled dialogue rewritten.

### M2 — "Dead Drop"
- **Objective:** cemetery by day; print #2 found at Jae-Ho's grave (different figure circled); Mandu enters with strictly bounded knowledge; Jae-Ho's apartment yields the Kostov courier note; escape under fire.
- **Gameplay:** grave examination, dialogue with Mandu (three approaches reveal different *sourcing*, not different facts), apartment search (evidence objects), pursuit/escape with Haru wounded (left-ear tinnitus — permanent, used M17).
- **Clue:** light-coffin sexton rumor (Mandu WITNESSED); Kostov name (apartment note); the two-circle detail (player-observable).
- **Consequence:** watchers now know the son digs; apartment burned as a site.
- **Transition:** Niko half-doubts Jae-Ho lives; "courier" leads to M3/M7.
- **Fixes:** Mandu no longer narrates the conspiracy (7.3); Beneficiary unnamed (7.4); Roman cut from night cemetery scene; headstone 2011.

### M3 — "Monitored"
- **Objective:** surveillance confirmed; the distorted call (the Accountant, voice-changed; never names the Beneficiary — threatens Roman's depot to set terms). Three-route intel operation with **exclusive** yields and **exclusive** costs.
- **Gameplay:** Route A lose-the-tail sequence (map the rotation, photograph plates); Route B trap-and-capture (containment fight, non-lethal option flagged); Route C Mandu's con (social-engineering minigame on the paymaster's courier).
- **Clue (exclusive per route):** A → rotation map + plates → watchers' safehouse (M8 site). B → captured watcher + the phrase-set spoken live ("the account is ready…") → Niko's burn (first phrase echo of the 2011 text). C → the paymaster's shell name → M6 evidence thread, and Mandu's cover burned with the network.
- **Consequence:** one exclusive lead + one exclusive cost logged; no route grants the others' intel (7.6 fix).
- **Transition:** the caller's parting line books M4: "You didn't come to the water. I'll bring the water to you."
- **Fixes:** caller ≠ Beneficiary (4.10); Niko recognizes *phrases*, not voice/face (Niko profile); Roman's danger established with agency framing.

### M4 — "The Man at the Docks"
- **Objective:** second lure accepted on Haru's terms — he stages the meet, controls the ground. The Accountant (voice-masked, lawyer-calm) lets him discover the stage was pre-controlled. First contact; the rules of the game; the torn ledger page.
- **Gameplay:** reverse-surveillance setup (player places overwatch, seals two exits — all anticipated), pier dialogue scene, containment scramble when third parties (network watchers) crash the meet, extraction across dockside container stacks.
- **Clue:** torn ledger page — a Seorin-era payment entry, signature block the family attorneys recognize (M5 hook). "I mailed the map. I need the knife."
- **Consequence:** Haru's control lesson #1 (flaw beaten publicly); watchers get a partial face for "the client's phone man."
- **Transition:** Min-ji calls — someone bid overnight on the dormant subsidiary. ACT BUTTON: Family Empire.

## ACT II — FAMILY EMPIRE (M5–M8)

### M5 — "Blood and Paper"
- **Objective:** Min-ji reunion; the doctrine war established; the 2011 expense note with both Tae-joon's and Niko's names (first document bridging father and protector).
- **Gameplay:** corporate interior scenes; records-room walkthrough tutorial for M6's mechanics; one hostile audit encounter (guards, non-lethal).
- **Clue:** expense note; Min-ji's filed trust notice (player sees it if attentive — fair-play for M15).
- **Consequence:** sibling pact with conditions: no violence inside her perimeter; Roman/Mallorie safety as a line item that never gets cut.

### M6 — "The Quiet Bid"
- **Objective:** the dormant-subsidiary acquisition fight. Whoever is bidding (the succession war's LC front) wants Seorin's legacy entities for what they still *paper*. Boardroom + records-room infiltration: Min-ji coins **"the Beneficiary"** — the account that only ever receives.
- **Gameplay:** social-stealth records infiltration (Mandu forgery assists), document-triage minigame, boardroom negotiation set-piece with a real lose-state (bad deal accepted = harder M13).
- **Clue:** the receiving-account pattern; the bidder's shell ties to Haeun's LC restructuring.
- **Consequence:** exposure package started (runs to M16); Mandu caught mid-forgery by Min-ji (respect earned).
- **Transition:** pattern needs a mouth: the crew's phrase-set from M3 now matters → M7.

### M7 — "The Photographer" *(path-flavored by M3 choice)*
- **Objective:** find the 2011 spotter whose insurance roll made the prints; land on Kostov's trade. Kostov talks (transactional, unnerved by "nobody said heirs").
- **Gameplay:** surveillance/drive work; an intimidation-then-talk sequence; skill checks flavored by M3 lead (A: plate map approach, B: captured-watcher leverage, C: shell-name pretext).
- **Clue:** courier chain + the liaison's carelessness ("the client never asked for the payment back").
- **Consequence:** Kostov flips quietly; network notices the leash slipping (heat +).
- **Transition:** courier chain intersects Roman's depot bookings → M8.

### M8 — "Meter and Fare"
- **Objective:** Roman's pattern discovery (his agency beat — binding); the watchers' safehouse raid; the depot locked down; Mallorie evacuation plan set.
- **Gameplay:** depot-defense setup, Roman-led briefing, safehouse infiltration/raid with extraction; failure states threaten the depot, not the family (family never on-screen endangered).
- **Clue:** rotation map converges with Kostov's courier chain; a watcher's phone shows one recurring appointment: "monthly reconciliation — E."
- **Consequence:** watchers' safehouse burned; network switches to direct action (escalation license for Act III).
- **Transition:** reconciliation appointments → the sexton's ledger → the light coffin. ACT BUTTON: The Man Who Lied.

## ACT III — THE MAN WHO LIED (M9–M11)

### M9 — "The Light Coffin"
- **Objective:** churchyard investigation: sexton's ledger, gravedigger's silence, the county morgue swap. Mandu's brother-seed surfaces (his 2011 loss touches Seorin's uncounted).
- **Gameplay:** investigation chain (interviews, records, one tail), quiet character scenes; zero combat — the act's breath.
- **Clue:** who helped Jae-Ho vanish (the swap's clean paperwork = a comptroller's signature style).
- **Consequence:** Mandu's real stake declared; group's trust geometry shifts.

### M10 — "What the Dead Man Knew"
- **Objective:** find Jae-Ho. The confession: what he booked, what he thought it meant, the four minutes, the warning that saved Haru and Min-ji. The PLANTED fact (a "verified" location) nearly kills them — Mandu's guilt scene; the patter drops.
- **Gameplay:** rural safehouse approach (stealth, one contested fight), long two-hander dialogue scene, trap sequence on exit.
- **Clue:** the forged letter's signature block; "I have the names" journal line finally paid off *specifically*.
- **Consequence:** Jae-Ho's existence now known to the network (watchers confirm during the trap); his clock starts.

### M11 — "Fourteen Years of Silence"
- **Objective:** the rupture. Confronted with Jae-Ho's confession and Min-ji's bookkeeper deduction, Haru demands everything; Niko sets the burner phone on the table (battery still out) and hands over fourteen years of silence in one object. The Accountant's fabrication is *deduced*, not confessed. Act ends: the network repossesses Jae-Ho — the doomsday file called in.
- **Gameplay:** dialogue-dominant mission with one extraction attempt (fail-forward scripted — the repossession must land).
- **Clue:** the burner's text ("ACCOUNT CLEARS FRIDAY. THEN THE BROOM.") → M12's clock.
- **Consequence:** Haru–Niko rupture (unresolved into Act IV); Jae-Ho taken; group rebuilds around certainty-seeking instead of control.
- **Transition:** the account that clears Friday + Haeun restructuring + print #1's face = the identification stack. ACT BUTTON: The Beneficiary.

## ACT IV — THE BENEFICIARY (M12–M14)

### M12 — "The Account That Clears Friday"
- **Objective:** the identification completes — fair-play stack: burner text ↔ ledger pattern ↔ Haeun LC restructuring ↔ liaison's face in print #1 ↔ forged signature block. The name lands: **Baek Seung-ho**. *(P-4.11 payoff.)*
- **Gameplay:** synthesis/investigation mission (the campaign's "board scene"), then a hard evidence grab at a Haeun records annex.
- **Clue:** every prior clue pays here; nothing new is introduced (fair-play rule: the reveal spends only planted coins).
- **Consequence:** Baek knows they know; the parley is scheduled — by him.
- **Transition:** the invitation arrives as courtesy. He does not do threats.

### M13 — "The Price of Everything"
- **Objective:** the parley. The offer: LC chair, debts dissolved, the past filed. The price: silence. Haru's flaw at high-water; Min-ji's inversion breaks it. Ends without violence — Baek schedules instead.
- **Gameplay:** dialogue set-piece (the campaign's signature scene), branching *posture* (defiant/cautious/hostile) recorded but not decision-locked; a controlled exit through a building that is politely a trap.
- **Consequence:** Haru's near-acceptance scars his self-trust (arc fuel); Min-ji accelerates the package.
- **Transition:** the counterstroke is already booked → M14.

### M14 — "The Long Error"
- **Objective:** Baek's counterstroke lands on three fronts in one night: the depot (Roman's people, not family on-screen), Mandu's identity kit burned, Jae-Ho's location raided for repossession-to-silence. The Accountant defects — delivers the master ledger's location. The location is bait.
- **Gameplay:** three-front defense (player picks two fronts, scripted allies cover the third — real cost either way), the defection meet, the trap revealed.
- **Clue:** "monthly reconciliation — E" pays; the ledger location's bait structure confirms Marsh's game.
- **Consequence:** costs are permanent by design (depot damage, Mandu exposed, a name lost to the network); trust geometry reformed around earned certainty.
- **Transition:** the trust unlocks. ACT BUTTON: SASIN.

## ACT V — SASIN (M15–M17)

### M15 — "The Testament of Kim Tae-joon"
- **Objective:** the trust delivers: the ledger in the father's hand — fifteen years of entries, the two refusals underlined, the final transaction, the recordings. The family holds its whole truth; Min-ji chooses exposure on her own terms; Haru must hold both truths of his father without worship or indictment.
- **Gameplay:** document/reading interludes, recordings (VO), the verdict scene; one protective action as the network makes its last play for the pages.
- **Clue:** the ledger's dates are checkable against everything since M2 — the campaign's fairness made physical.
- **Consequence:** package complete; exposure scheduled around the succession congress.
- **Transition:** everyone's price is now known; one man's isn't.

### M16 — "Every Man Has a Price"
- **Objective:** assembly of the reckoning: evidence to regulators and the journalist; the factions priced and flipped; Roman drives the night's logistics; Mandu's offer-and-refusal ("You had one product I never sold, chairman: myself"); Niko's accounting with Haru (the Kate speech — not absolution, certainty).
- **Gameplay:** negotiation/faction scenes, courier protection runs, the quiet before the congress.
- **Clue:** Baek's attempted purchase of the package (he tries to *buy* the exposure twice — blind spot #2 on display).
- **Consequence:** partners primed; the congress is set; every ally's choice is made on-screen.
- **Transition:** the harbor offices. The room where killing is easy.

### M17 — "SASIN"
- **Objective:** the succession congress at the docks (bookend of the "—A." lure). Exposure detonates through Min-ji's channels mid-session; the partners remove the Chairman; Haru holds the kill and declines it — *"I don't need you dead. I need you certain you've lost."* Epilogues: Jae-Ho tends the churchyard; Mandu's brother's claim is filed with Min-ji's help; Niko goes bowling with Roman; the house survives, smaller, governed.
- **Gameplay:** infiltration to position, timed exposure sequence, the confrontation scene, controlled ending scenes; combat optional and de-emphasized — the flaw's final lesson is that this room never needed his gun.
- **Clue:** none — the act is all payoff; tinnitus beat (he cannot hear his own heartbeat in the choice).
- **Consequence:** THE RECKONING delivered (P-5.5); SASIN becomes a rumor that testifies.
- **Transition:** campaign end; post-game epilogue scenes.

## Pacing & variety check (Stage 7 criteria)
- **Combat-light acts:** I (1 fight), III (2), V (optional) — investigation/drama carry; **combat peaks:** M8, M14. Variety across: surveillance (M3), stealth-infiltration (M6, M12), pursuit (M2), social-engineering (M3C, M6), corporate negotiation (M5, M6, M13), investigation chains (M9, M12), defense (M8, M14), dialogue set-pieces (M10, M11, M13, M16, M17).
- **Every act hurts permanently once:** M2 (Haru's ear), M8 (depot heat), M11 (Niko rupture, Jae-Ho taken), M14 (Mandu exposed, losses), M17 (the father's full truth).
- **No filler audit:** each mission above names its advanced element; any future mission added to reach 18 must displace one, not pad.

## Single-canon ending policy
Branches alter **paths, costs, and texture**, never the destination (M3 routes, M6 lose-state, M13 posture, M14 front-choice). The ending is single-canon (The Reckoning) because the campaign's argument — certainty over control, exposure over leverage — must be *earned in one shape*. Rationale documented; a multi-ending structure remains possible only by user direction.
