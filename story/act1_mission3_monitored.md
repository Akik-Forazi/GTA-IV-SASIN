# ACT I — MISSION 3: "Monitored"

## Mission Overview
**Placement:** Act I, Mission 3 of 3  
**Dramatic Objective:** Someone is monitoring the survivors; Haru discovers evidence of surveillance; the mystery of the anonymous photograph deepens; a major decision point about how to proceed.  
**Why It Matters Now:** This is the climax of Act I — the "someone is watching" thread from Mission 1 is confirmed and expanded. Haru now knows he's being actively monitored, which raises the stakes and forces a decision: run, fight, or investigate more carefully.  
**Character Conflict:** Haru's instinct vs. Niko's experience; Roman's vulnerability as a known associate; Mandu's performance vs. genuine concern for Roman's safety.  
**Gameplay Variety:** Surveillance detection mini-game, decision point, character conversation, brief infiltration, mission bifurcation based on player choice.

## Gameplay Flow

1. **Safehouse scene** — Haru, Niko, and the photograph; the mysterious phone call from the "Beneficiary's" representative  
2. **Dialogue options** — Haru's response to the threat (defiant, cautious, aggressive) affects what follows:  
   - **Defiant:** "I'm not for sale." → Leads to **Option B (set a trap)**  
   - **Cautious:** "We need to be smarter." → Leads to **Option C (contact Mandu)**  
   - **Aggressive:** "Let him come." → Leads to **Option A (try to lose the watchers)** but with higher risk  
3. **Player exits the safehouse** into the rainy streets of Bohan  
4. **Surveillance detection mini-game** — the player must avoid or lose observers:  
   - Three black sedans are following Haru (scripted traffic AI that actively follows the player)  
   - Player must use: changing routes, entering interiors (safehouses, shops), losing tail techniques (pay-n-spray, garage)  
   - If caught: a cutscene triggers — Haru is surrounded, but Niko intervenes (or doesn't, depending on player choices)  
   - If lost: mission progresses to the next phase  
5. **Decision point reached** — player chooses one of three paths:  

### PATH A: Lose the Watchers (Direct Confrontation)
- **Gameplay:** Brief pursuit/lose-tail sequence through Bohan streets  
- **Outcome:** Haru loses the sedans in traffic, but realizes they were *letting* him go — a message left in his car: a business card with a single name: **VLADIMIR KOSTOV** written on the back  
- **Dialogue:** Niko "They let you go on purpose. They're not hunting you. They're *inviting* you."  
- **Mandu (if contacted earlier):** "Clever. They want you to *chase* them. To *prove* you're looking. Don't take the bait."

### PATH B: Set a Trap (Stealth/Infiltration)
- **Gameplay:** Player chooses a location (warehouse district, Mandu's known haunt, Jae-Ho's old apartment) and sets up surveillance  
- **Outcome:** Mandu uses deception to lure a surveillance team into a controlled encounter — they capture a low-level Russian enforcer who reveals he's been *hired* by someone using the Beneficiary's name, but the enforcer doesn't know the Beneficiary's *real* identity  
- **Dialogue:** Captured enforcer "He gave me a name. A company. International something-or-other. But he... he never saw a face. Just... voice. On the phone. Always the same phrases. 'The account is ready.' 'The asset is secure.' 'The problem is handled.'"

### PATH C: Contact Mandu (Deception/Manipulation)
- **Gameplay:** Player seeks out Mandu at his known hangout (a karaoke bar or underground club in Chinatown/BO area)  
- **Outcome:** Mandu uses impersonation and social engineering to gain access to whoever is monitoring Haru — he discovers it's not the Beneficiary directly, but a *lieutenant* who believes he's working for a routine "cleanup" operation  
- **Dialogue:** Mandu "My friends... they're just doing jobs. They don't know the big picture. Nobody does. Except... perhaps the Accountant. The one who makes the numbers disappear."  
- **New objective:** "Meet the Accountant. Or discover who the Accountant really is."

6. **Regardless of path**, the mission converges on a shared revelation:  
   - The surveillance is real, coordinated, and comes from the network  
   - The Beneficiary is *actively* watching, not passively  
   - Roman is a known vulnerability — the surveillance mentions him by connection to Haru  
   - A name emerges: **VLADIMIR KOSTOV** or **THE ACCOUNTANT** — a new intermediary to investigate  
   - The question of *why* the Beneficiary is revealing himself now — what has changed?

## Character Beats

- **Haru:** Defiant but calculating; the realization that the Beneficiary is actively watching changes his calculus — he can't just move blindly anymore; he needs information, not just revenge  
- **Niko:** Experienced, world-weary; his warning about "some hills aren't worth dying on" carries real weight now that the Beneficiary has entered the conversation; he's seen this movie before and knows how it ends badly  
- **Roman:** Vulnerability personified — he's the leverage point the network will use; his innocent presence in the safehouse contrasts with the danger outside; he doesn't understand the depth of the threat yet  
- **Mandu:** His competence as a deceiver is validated if contacted; his concern for Roman shows his growing loyalty; his performance is starting to crack in genuinely serious moments

## Escalation & Complication

- **The Beneficiary reveals himself actively** — the phone call proves he's not just a name on a page; he's watching, he's waiting, he's engaged  
- **Roman is identified as a vulnerability** — the surveillance mentions him; this raises the personal stakes from "Haru's revenge" to "protecting the people I love"  
- **Three distinct paths emerge** based on player choice, each revealing different pieces of the puzzle:  
  - **Path A** reveals the Beneficiary is *inviting* Haru to play — Kostov is the bait  
  - **Path B** reveals a hired enforcer who doesn't know the Beneficiary's real identity — the network uses cutouts  
  - **Path C** reveals a lieutenant who's being manipulated, and a shadowy figure called "The Accountant"  
- **All paths converge** on the same truth: the network is active, the Beneficiary is watching, and Roman is the leverage point  
- **The decision has consequences** for Act II — which path the player chooses determines what evidence, allies, and complications they bring forward

## Technical Implementation Notes

- **Safehouse interior:** Existing GTA IV safehouse (use the one already placed in the campaign)  
- **Phone terminal:** Scripted prop — modified cellphone with incoming call functionality  
- **Phone call voice:** Distorted/synthetic voice effect; use existing GTA IV radio/modulation scripts  
- **Three sedans following:** Scripted traffic AI that follows the player; can be lost using standard GTA IV lose-tail techniques (enter interior, pay-n-spray, garage, change clothes)  
- **Surveillance detection mini-game:** Use existing GTA wanted level / wanted star mechanics adapted for "surveillance" rather than police  
- **Player choice branching:** Standard GTA mission branching (three dialogue options or three route choices) leading to different but thematically consistent endings  
- **Business card item:** Scripted inventory item (Vladimir Kostov business card) that appears in Path A  
- **Captured enforcer (Path B):** Scripted non-hostile ped who gives dialogue when approached; uses existing GTA IV pedestrian radio/pedestrian assets  
- **Mandu's hangout (Path C):** Existing club/bar interior asset in Chinatown or Bohan  
- **LuaModLoader compatibility:** Uses existing native functions for phone calls, dialogue branching, traffic AI, and simple gameplay mechanics  
- **No custom weapons or vehicles required** — use existing GTA IV assets  
- **Cutscene triggers:** Standard GTA IV mission transition scripts
