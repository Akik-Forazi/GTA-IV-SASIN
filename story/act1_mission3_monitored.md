## Mission Overview
- **Placement:** Act I, Mission 3 of 4. **Dramatic objective:** surveillance is confirmed and *answered* — the group chooses one of three ways to hit back, and the choice buys one exclusive lead at one exclusive cost.

## The planning board (exclusive routes)

**HARU** "Three ways to answer a phone call."

- **Yield (EXCLUSIVE):** the rotation map — a six-block cadence, shift gaps, and the plates' registered keeper: a logistics firm that connects to the depot's corporate account. *(Feeds M7 approach A and pre-marks M8's safehouse.)*
- **Cost (EXCLUSIVE):** the network confirms Haru is hunting → heavier patrols M5–M7; one extra tail beat in each.
- **Scene button:** Niko, reviewing the plates: "They're not hiding because they're not hunting. Somebody *scheduled* this. Professionals on a calendar."

## Route B — "Catch a Hand" (trap and contain)
- **Gameplay:** stage the false drop (player places two points), containment fight — non-lethal takedowns flagged (`sasin_restraint`), one scripted handoff interruption. Failure = the watcher dies in the scuffle (flag `sasin_bodycount`, heavier M7).
