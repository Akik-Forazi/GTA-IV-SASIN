# Kim Min-ji

**Adoptive sister; corporate strategist.** Status refs: canon-ledger 1.7, 1.17, 2.x. Age 35 (b. 1984). Biological daughter of Kim Tae-joon. CONFIRMED name (pre-existing ledger).

## Player-facing definition
- Runs the successor holdings of the Seorin Group estate from LC (since 2017): winding down litigation, defending the subsidiaries, keeping the name clean. Brilliant at acquisitions, contracts, records, and reputation — the four levers of her world.
- Her doctrine is explicit and quotable: **"Information you fire is a weapon. Information you hold is insurance. Information you release is neither."**

## Voice
- Boardroom cadence even at a diner booth. Speaks in structures: options, terms, exposure. Warmth expressed through *precision* — she remembers your numbers, which is her love language. Her humor is scalpel-dry, and it lands hardest on Haru because he cannot preempt it.

## Flaw
**Believes controlling information prevents catastrophe.** Her father's secret-keeping is her original sin: she was the audit that found nothing because she was allowed to find nothing. Her arc inverts Haru's — where he must learn uncertainty, she must learn that some truths detonate on their own schedule, and controlling the *release* is not the same as owning the *truth*.

## Want / need
- Want (stated): keep Seorin's legitimate survivors alive and its people employed.
- Need (unstated): to stop doing her father's lying for him. The M6 discovery (the account pattern) and M15 delivery (the trust) force her to choose between the estate and the truth — and she chooses truth **on her own terms**, through counsel, records, and press — the strategist's revenge, not the reaper's.

## Arc by act
- **I — The Return**: off-page but present in texture: her firm's name on the subsidiary documents Haru touches (M4's torn page is a Seorin page — the first act button pulls her onstage).
- **II — Family Empire** (M5–M8): the doctrine war with Haru. M6: her analysis of the payment structure **coins the term "the Beneficiary"** — an account that only ever receives. She wants the pattern; she does not want her brother's war. Her condition: no violence inside her corporate perimeter.
- **III**: the rupture with Niko vindicates her doctrine for a day; Jae-Ho's confession breaks it for good — secrets she "controlled" were already owned by the enemy.
- **IV**: refuses the parley's terms before Haru does (M13) — she is the first to see that Baek is not offering control but *purchase of silence*. Begins assembling the exposure package as a hedge.
- **V — SASIN**: the reckoning is hers as much as Haru's: the package detonates through counsel, regulators, and the succession war's own parties. She faces the estate's compromise in full and rebuilds what can be rebuilt — her ending is the campaign's argument that exposure, priced honestly, is survivable.

## Relationships
- **Haru**: she audits him like a subsidiary and loves him like the brother who made her father's last decade warm. Neither says the second part; both act on it.
- **Tae-joon (deceased)**: the father she defended to the world and interrogates to herself. The trust (M15) is his last conversation with her.
- **Roman/Mallorie**: she treats their safety as a *line item that must never be cut* — her condition for cooperating with Haru.
- **The Beneficiary**: she never meets him until the end — and by then she has already beaten him in the only forum he never learned to price: records he did not know existed.

## Writer-only notes
- She receives the same trust notice in January 2019 and files it. Her guilt about filing it is her M5 tell — Haru reads his sister for the first time and is unsettled that he can.
- She never meets the Accountant; she deduces his existence from double-entry logic ("every fabrication needs a bookkeeper") — M11, her cleanest deduction, and the player's too.
- Her exposure package is assembled from M6 onward in pieces the player watches: never a last-minute miracle.

## Continuity guards
- She was at the warehouse that night (closing paperwork) — she carries the sound memory, used once, M15, quietly.
- She does not know the Beneficiary's identity before M12, and never uses the word "SASIN" for her brother in dialogue: to her he is "my brother," and the gap is the point.
