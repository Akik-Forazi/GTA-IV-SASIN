# Niko Bellic

**Survivor-protector; keeper of the concealed evidence.** Status refs: canon-ledger 1.6, 2.1, 2.3; chronology B7. GTA IV canon character — voice, history, and fate are locked to the **Revenge ending**.

## Canon-locked facts (never revise)
- Kate McReary **died** at Roman's wedding because Niko chose revenge on Dimitri Rascalov. Niko lives with that arithmetic daily. Kate is referenced in the **past tense only**; no dialogue may imply the Deal ending.
- Roman survived; Niko is uncle-figure to Roman and Mallorie's daughter.
- Post-2009 he keeps to quiet work and quieter loyalties: taxi depot shares, an unlisted number, bowling alone.

## The SASIN chapter (2009)
- Early 2009, referred by Packie McReary, Niko takes discreet security work for Kim Tae-joon — "pays clean, asks quiet." March 9, 2009: the warehouse. He kills two of the crew in the loading corridor and carries Haru and Min-ji out through the freight cut. He passes the suited liaison's body at the door and takes the man's **burner phone** — instinct, first; meaning, later **[P-4.7]**.

## Knowledge boundaries (binding — canon-ledger 2.3) **[P-4.8]**
- **Witnessed**: the suited liaison at the warehouse door, directing the crew without lifting a weapon; the crew's military splice and suppressed weapons (contract-grade, not street); the "loose hands" chaos of the massacre.
- **Inferred**: professional contract job; someone above the crew; someone above the man at the door. He does *not* know who.
- **Concealed**: the burner phone (battery out, Middle Park East cash locker, ten years) and the one text he read on it in 2009: **"ACCOUNT CLEARS FRIDAY. THEN THE BROOM."**; the liaison's description (build, age, expensive coat, slim wedding band).
- **Does not know**: the Beneficiary, the Accountant, the ledger, the trust, that the children's survival was falsified paperwork, or that Jae-Ho lives (until M2's evidence, and even then he half-doubts it until M9).

## Why he concealed it (the credible choice)
He began tracing the account reference in 2009 and stopped at the point the trail could touch Roman. Then the wedding happened, and the arithmetic of grief closed the ledger on curiosity: *he could not save Kate by knowing everything; he will not lose Roman and the two survivors by trying.* The player should understand him even where Haru cannot forgive him.

## Voice
- Wry, fatalistic, precise about violence and vague about feelings. Deflects with domestic detail (bowling, the depot, Roman's diet). When cornered emotionally, he goes literal — short sentences, no metaphors. Grief speaks in objects: he tends Kate-related things carefully (a bowling league jacket he never wears, an anniversary he marks alone).

## Arc by act
- **I**: obligation answered in person: he shows up, watches, warns ("some doors, once opened, don't close nicely"). His M3 recognition is of **phrases only** ("the account is ready" echoes the burner text) — never a face, never a name. REJECTED by ledger 7.x: Niko recognizing the Beneficiary's voice.
- **II**: assists within his terms: no Roman exposure, no families touched. He walks the M7 scene with Haru and identifies the crew's tradecraft — what he can testify to, he does.
- **III — The Man Who Lied** (M11): the rupture. Confronted with Jae-Ho's confession and the Accountant's fabrication, Haru demands everything. Niko sets the phone on the table, battery still out, and says only: "It was in the man's pocket. I read one message. Then I was a coward for ten years." **He does not narrate his decade; he hands over the object.** The rupture is real and unresolved at act's end.
- **IV**: chooses to stand — not for forgiveness, but because the phone was *his* choice and the reckoning will be too. His tradecraft wins M14's counterstroke planning.
- **V**: the accounting (M16): he tells Haru what he never told anyone about the night Kate died — not for absolution, but because certainty is the only gift he has left to give. Epilogue: uneasy, honest, standing. He does not attend the aftermath press conference; he goes bowling with Roman.

## Relationships
- **Haru**: the son-shaped debt. He was there for the rescue; he chose silence over certainty; he pays it across the whole campaign.
- **Roman**: the line Niko will not let anyone cross — the reason he stayed silent and the reason he finally stands.
- **Kate**: the wound that shaped the choice; referenced with tenderness, never exposition.
- **Mandu**: Niko distrusts him on principle and is never wrong to, and is still wrong about him by the end.

## Continuity guards
- Never says "the Beneficiary" or "the Accountant" before M12 (and after M12, only as titles learned from others).
- Never claims to recognize a face or voice from 2009 except the liaison's, and he never claims *that* in dialogue until print #1 is on the table (M12 scene).
- His violence stays competent, not superhuman; the SASIN gap (Haru's 10x baseline) is visible in every fight they share.
