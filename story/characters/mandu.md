# Mandu (Moon Du-seok)

**Con artist; bounded-knowledge comic; real loyalty.** Status refs: canon-ledger 1.8, 2.4, 2.5. Age 39 (b. 1980, Incheon). CONFIRMED: original character, humor without Deadpool-clone energy. PROPOSED: real name/nickname origin.

## Player-facing definition
- A working con artist of the first rank: impersonation, bluff, social engineering, improvisation. Broke more often than he admits, generous exactly when it costs him.
- "Mandu" — dumpling — is the nickname from his first grift (selling freezer-burned mandu on credit outside a Busan shipyard at 19). He tells four versions of the story; one is true; he has forgotten which.

## Voice
- Runs patter like a card mechanic runs a deck — the joke is *cover*, not personality. He narrates cons as they happen ("you're watching the left hand, which is fair, it's the one I practiced"). Key design rule (canon 1.8): **when the stakes turn real, the patter thins** — the audience should hear the difference. His sincerity, when it finally arrives, is devastating *because* the comedy was load-bearing.

## Knowledge boundaries (binding — canon 2.4)
Every Mandu fact must be taggable to one of five sources. Writers add to this table, never improvise past it:
| Tag | Meaning | Examples (Act I) |
|-----|---------|------------------|
| WITNESSED | he saw/heard it himself | the sexton of the Bohan churchyard once drunkenly told him the coffin was "light as a suitcase" (M2) |
| INFERRED | pattern-matching from his trade | staged deaths are bought from the inside; nobody fakes a funeral alone (M2) |
| BLUFF | said to move the scene; may be false | his "I know who left the photograph" approach gambit (M2 — he doesn't; he watched Haru's face) |
| RUMOR | street word of unknown reliability | a shell outfit paying cab fares in cash (M6 lead, half right) |
| PLANTED | *someone fed it to him* — dangerous | none yet in Act I; his first PLANTED fact arrives in Act III and nearly kills the group |

- In Act I Mandu does **not** know: the Beneficiary (name, title, or philosophy), the Accountant, the ledger, the trust, or who sent the photographs. He knows Jae-Ho's death smells wrong, he knows the watchers' bread pattern (he has been circling the same crumbs), and he knows Haru is the biggest prey to walk into his best hunt.

## The hidden stake (seeded M2/M6, revealed M9–M10) **[P-chronology E15]**
His older brother, **Moon Du-hyun**, was a Seorin warehouseman on the March 2009 night shift — one of the eleven, and one of the *uncounted*: paid as a temp through a shell, never listed, never claimed. The compensation fund the attorneys set up missed him entirely. Mandu has spent ten years hunting the payment chain with the only tools he has — and he is good with tools.

## Arc by act
- **I**: enters M2 working his own angle (the sexton lead), invites himself into the hunt, proves useful, gets under every door he shouldn't. Comic engine; real blade underneath.
- **II**: his forgeries open doors Haru's money can't (M6 records room); his rumor network supplies the M3 Route C method. Haru starts staging him into plans; Mandu starts *letting* him, which is its own con.
- **III**: the PLANTED fact — a "verified" location for Jae-Ho that is actually a network trap (fed through his RUMOR chain). Mandu's guilt when it breaks (M10) is the first time the audience sees him without patter. His confession of the brother (M9–M10) reframes every joke he has told.
- **IV**: Baek's counterstroke burns his identity kit (M14) — the man of ten faces has none left. He keeps working with nothing but nerve.
- **V**: the offer (M16): Baek prices him like everyone else. Mandu — broke, hunted, out of costumes — declines. "You had one product I never sold, chairman: myself. Turns out it's not for sale." Baek's composure cracks on that line more than on the exposure. (Chronology Part D; P-5.5.)

## Relationships
- **Haru**: the straight man Mandu never expected to respect. Their comedy is method conflict: staging vs. improvisation.
- **Niko**: mutual professional distrust that curdles into brotherhood at the exact moment both stop performing.
- **Min-ji**: she is the only person who has ever caught him mid-con (M6) — he has never forgiven her, and has asked her twice to teach him.
- **The brother**: the wound under the patter; his compensation claim (refused, refiled in the epilogue with Min-ji's help) is the campaign's quietest justice.

## Continuity guards
- Not a Deadpool clone: no fourth-wall, no meta. The humor is *in-world*, situational, and deflates when stakes demand.
- He never solves the mystery before the evidence does; his intel is always tagged, and at least once per act a tag is proven wrong.
- He is competent, not magical: two of his cons fail on-screen, one expensively.
