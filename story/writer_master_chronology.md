# Writer-Only Master Chronology — GTA IV: SASIN

*This document reconstructs the underlying truth chronologically. It distinguishes what actually happened from what individual characters believe. Unresolved facts are labeled OPEN. This is not player-facing — do not distribute.*

## PRE-MASSACRE HISTORY (Bangladesh / Korea / Liberty City)

### The Adoptive Family's Origin
- **Ahsan** was born in Bangladesh. Adopted by a powerful Korean family (surname Kim) and raised as **Kim Haru**.
- The adoptive family operated a legitimate weapons business AND maintained a corporate infrastructure that served as a front for a shadow network.
- The network (provisionally **Operation Black Ledger**) handled transactions, shell companies, criminal intermediaries, and influential beneficiaries.

### The Network's True Purpose (OPEN — exact nature undecided)
- The network was originally a weapons-supply chain for regional conflicts.
- The adoptive father discovered it was being used for **extrajudicial executions and "cleanup" operations** targeting witnesses and competitors.
- **OPEN**: What specific conflict or state was the network supporting? What was the "something substantially worse" the father discovered?

### The Adoptive Father's Compromises
- He made **questionable business compromises** and benefited from the dangerous commercial network for years.
- His earlier compromises helped create the conditions that made the disaster possible.
- He eventually discovered the network's true use and **attempted to stop or dismantle the dangerous operation**.
- **OPEN**: What was his final transaction? What did he do that "made matters worse"?

## THE MASSACRE (circa 2008, date unspecified)

### What Happened
- Russian criminals physically carried out the massacre at a warehouse in Liberty City.
- The Russian crew was **expendable**; someone more powerful manipulated the incentives, access, information, and deniable intermediaries behind the operation.
- **The intermediary**: Someone arranged access, money, information, or logistics. Jae-Ho Lee was Niko's contact who arranged the Russian crew.

### Niko's Role
- Niko Bellic served as the adoptive father's bodyguard.
- He **rescued Haru and his adoptive sister** from the warehouse during the massacre.
- **Niko witnessed an intermediary's identity** during the massacre. He **concealed a fragment of evidence** that identified this intermediary.
- **Why he concealed it**: He believed revealing the information would endanger the survivors (Haru, his sister, Roman). He chose to protect the people he had left, especially after Kate's death in the Revenge ending.
- **Niko's central flaw** (deciding what other people need to know) is exercised here — a credible choice the player can understand, even if Haru later feels betrayed.

### What the Russians Knew and Why They Attacked
- The Russian criminals were the **visible perpetrators**. They may have believed they were executing a routine cleanup operation.
- **OPEN**: Did the Russians know the full extent of the network's criminality, or were they manipulated through false flags and deniable intermediaries?
- The Beneficiary's people arranged the logistics and deniability; the Russians executed the operation.

### Haru and Sister's Survival
- Haru and his sister **survived the massacre** through Niko's intervention.
- **OPEN**: Specific circumstances of their survival beyond "Niko rescued them." Why were they spared while others died?
- The network helped its people disappear — Jae-Ho Lee's faked death is evidence of this.

## IMMEDIATE AFTERMATH (2008–2018)

### Jae-Ho Lee's Fate
- Jae-Ho Lee **faked his death** with help from someone inside the network.
- The grave at the cemetery contains another man's body — someone who looked like Jae-Ho.
- **Jae-Ho went into hiding**, fed small pieces of information to keep himself alive but not enough to help him expose the network.
- **OPEN**: Who helped Jae-Ho fake his death? (Niko? The father? Someone else? The scripts hint it was "someone in the network" but don't specify.)

### The Sister's Arc (Beginning)
- She survived the massacre and became an **independent corporate strategist** who controls or leads the family's legitimate business empire.
- Her skills: corporate strategy, acquisitions, contracts, financial records, institutional relationships, reputation management.
- **Her flaw**: Believing that controlling information can prevent catastrophe.
- **Her arc** (future): Deciding whether protecting the family's legitimate business requires exposing the family's compromised past.
- **OPEN**: Her precise name, age, personality details are undecided.

### The Adoptive Father's Later Life
- He continued to lead the family's legitimate business while secretly attempting to dismantle part of the network.
- **OPEN**: His exact actions between the massacre and the campaign's start. What was the "final transaction"?

## THE CAMPAIGN'S OPENING (2018, post-GTA IV)

### Haru's Situation
- Haru has lived in Liberty City (or abroad) and returns after receiving **an anonymous photograph** related to the massacre.
- He is **wealthy, connected, and highly capable** — his combat design target is approximately ten times ordinary baseline, but he is not immortal.
- His central flaw: **Confusing control with certainty**.
- He becomes known in the underworld as **SASIN** (death-bringing figure / Grim Reaper).

### Niko's Situation
- Niko is living in Liberty City, has **concealed the evidence fragment** identifying the intermediary.
- He has not revealed this to Haru, believing it would endanger him.
- He is guarded, practical, shaped by experience (including Kate's death and Roman's survival).
- When Haru returns, Niko is **reluctant but obligated** to re-engage.

### The Beneficiary's Situation
- The Beneficiary is a **respected international business figure** who believes everyone has a price.
- He does not personally order every event — he sets conditions (corrupt officials, accessible intermediaries, deniable assets) that make operations possible.
- He **has been monitoring Haru** for some time before the campaign starts (Mission 3 confirms he "always knows when someone comes back").
- **His blind spots**: He underestimates those who refuse to be bought; he manipulates through deniable intermediaries, not direct action.
- **OPEN**: His specific blind spots that the protagonists can exploit. What does he misunderstand about Haru?

## ACT I MISSIONS — WHAT HAPPENED (as revealed in the scripts)

### Mission 1: "The Return"
- Haru arrives at BO Hann International Airport.
- He receives an **anonymous text**: "THEY'RE WATCHING. MEET ME AT THE DOCKS. —A."
- He senses he's being watched (black sedan).
- He contacts Niko — Niko is surprised but agrees to meet.
- Roman is at the safehouse in Bohan, excited about Haru's return.
- **Clue placed**: The anonymous photograph (physically in Haru's possession; text references it).
- **Niko's concealed evidence is hinted at** but not described ("some doors, once opened, don't close nicely").
- **Roman survives** and is present; Kate's death is referenced.

### Mission 2: "Dead Drop"
- Haru and Niko visit Jae-Ho Lee's grave in a Bohan cemetery.
- **Reveal**: Jae-Ho faked his death — the grave contains another man's body.
- **Mandu** appears; he introduces the Beneficiary's philosophy: "everyone has a price."
- **Vladimir Kostov** is introduced as a name found in Jae-Ho's apartment (a photograph of the massacre with Kostov's name on the back).
- **Niko's concealed evidence is paralleled** with Jae-Ho's choice: both chose to protect loved ones rather than expose the truth.
- **The Russians are shown circling the cemetery** — they're monitoring survivors.
- **Three options** for the player, but gameplay leads to Jae-Ho's old apartment.
- **Apartment search reveals**: Jae-Ho's journal, a hidden compartment with a photograph (Vladimir Kostov), financial records, a recording of Jae-Ho explaining he was forced to fake his death.
- **Jae-Ho's journal fragment** reads: "'...the network is not just weapons. It is... disposal. Cleanup. The Russians are the visible hand, but someone else moves the strings. I have the names. But if I use them... they will not go after me. They will go after the people I love. Roman. Kate's memory. You, Haru.'"
- **Mission ending**: Knock at the apartment door; surveillance team approaching; Niko insists they must leave.

### Mission 3: "Monitored"
- At the safehouse, Haru, Niko, and the photograph review the warehouse massacre image.
- ** major escalation**: The Beneficiary **calls directly** — a distorted, synthetic voice says: "You're being watched, Mr. Haru. Not just by us. By *him*. The Beneficiary. He knows you're back. He always knows when someone comes back."
- **Roman is identified as a vulnerability** — the surveillance mentions him as leverage.
- **Three player-choice paths** converge on the same core truth:
  - **Path A (Lose Watchers)**: Business card with **Vladimir Kostov** written on it, phrase "The account is ready." Niko recognizes Kostov as a name from his past.
  - **Path B (Set Trap)**: Mandu lures a surveillance team; they capture a low-level Russian enforcer who reveals he was hired by someone using the Beneficiary's name but **never saw a face** — only voice on phone. Phrases: "The account is ready." "The asset is secure." "The problem is handled."
  - **Path C (Contact Mandu)**: Mandu uses deception to discover it's not the Beneficiary directly, but a **lieutenant** who believes he's working for a routine "cleanup" operation. There's a shadowy figure called "**The Accountant**" who makes the numbers disappear.
- **All paths converge**: Surveillance is real, coordinated, from the network. Roman is the leverage point. The Beneficiary is actively watching, not passive.
- **Decision has consequences** for Act II — which path the player chose determines what evidence/allies/complications they bring forward.

## CHRONOLOGICAL SUMMARY (What Actually Happened)

1. Adoptive family's weapons business and corporate infrastructure become connected to Operation Black Ledger (network of transactions, shell companies, criminal intermediaries, influential beneficiaries).

2. Adoptive father makes compromises, benefits from the network, eventually discovers it's being used for extrajudicial executions/"cleanup" operations.

3. Father attempts to stop/dismantle the dangerous operation. His earlier compromises help create conditions the disaster becomes possible.

4. Russian criminals attack the family massacre at a warehouse. Someone more powerful manipulates incentives, access, information, deniable intermediaries behind the operation.

5. Niko Bellic (as father's bodyguard) rescues Haru and his sister. He conceals a fragment of evidence identifying an intermediary because he believes revealing it would endanger survivors. Kate dies; Roman survives (Revenge ending).

6. Jae-Ho Lee fakes his death with help from someone in the network. The network helps its people disappear.

7. 10 years later: Haru receives anonymous photograph, returns to Liberty City. Niko has concealed the evidence fragment all this time.

8. The Beneficiary has been monitoring Haru; he "always knows when someone comes back." He operates through lieutenants and cutouts.

9. Act I missions: Haru's return, Jae-Ho revealed alive, Beneficiary actively calls, Roman as surveillance target, three investigation paths emerge for Act II.

10. The evidence resurfaces now because the Beneficiary has changed his approach — he's no longer waiting in the wings. Something has shifted that makes him active in the present.
